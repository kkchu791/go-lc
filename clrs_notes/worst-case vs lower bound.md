### Worst case vs Lower Bound

Worst-case input scenario - sequence of operations designed to make the algo work as hard as possible (making the tree as tall as possible)

Lower bound ( horse shoe or "at least") - refers to mathematical guarantee on time:

"Even under the smartest analyzing(smartest coders), this bad scenario is guaranteed to take AT LEAST this much time.
